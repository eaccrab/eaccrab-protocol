# EACCRAB Whitepaper

## Vision

Markets reward the ones who arrive early and stay light. EACCRAB takes the crab as its emblem: it does not walk straight at a crowd, it moves sideways and lands first. The project exists to give the Elysium community a recognisable mascot, an honest live view of the chain's pools, and a home that never pretends to know more than it does.

## Mission

1. Be useful on day one: show live Elysium pool data whether or not the project's own token has launched.
2. Be honest by construction: every external fact is a single record, and anything not confirmed is displayed as empty.
3. Stay light: a static site with no tracking, no framework, and nothing to maintain beyond one register file.
